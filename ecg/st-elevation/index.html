Ischemia and infarction

ST elevation

ST elevation in contiguous leads. The differential is wider than occlusion: pericarditis, early repolarization, left ventricular aneurysm, Brugada, hyperkalemia and left bundle branch block all produce it. Reciprocal depression is the finding that most favors occlusion.

12 tracings, each labelled STE_ (non-specific ST elevation) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.
