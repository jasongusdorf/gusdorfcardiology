Rhythm

Atrial premature complexes

An early P wave of different morphology from the sinus P, conducting to a normal narrow QRS, usually followed by an incomplete compensatory pause. A very early atrial beat can find the bundle refractory and conduct with aberrancy, which is the commonest reason a supraventricular beat looks ventricular.
