Conduction

Second degree AV block

Some P waves conduct and some do not. Mobitz I shows progressive PR lengthening before a dropped beat and usually sits in the node. Mobitz II drops beats without warning and sits below it, which is why Mobitz II is the one that earns a pacemaker.

12 tracings, each labelled 2AVB (second degree AV block) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.
