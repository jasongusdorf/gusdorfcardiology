Electrolyte disturbance or drug effect

Repolarization change attributable to electrolytes or medication. Peaked T waves and QRS widening with hyperkalemia, U waves and a long QU with hypokalemia, a short QT with hypercalcemia. The ECG is faster than the chemistry panel, which is the entire reason to look.

12 tracings, each labelled EL (electrolytic disturbance or drug (former EDIS)) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.
