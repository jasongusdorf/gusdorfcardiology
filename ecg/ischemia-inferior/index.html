Ischemia and infarction

Ischemia, inferior leads

Ischemic ST-T change in II, III and aVF. Right coronary territory in most people, left circumflex in those with a dominant circumflex.

12 tracings, each labelled ISCIN (ischemic in inferior leads) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.

Source and licence

Every tracing here is rendered from raw signal in PTB-XL v1.0.3, an open dataset of clinical 12-lead ECGs, and is used under CC BY 4.0. Diagnostic labels are the dataset's cardiologist-assigned SCP-ECG statements, not my own reading and not a machine's.
