Ischemia and infarction

T wave inversion

Inverted T waves outside the leads where inversion is normal, being III, aVR and V1. Deep symmetric inversion across the anterior leads raises the question of critical proximal left anterior descending stenosis. T inversion is also the expected secondary change wherever depolarization is abnormal, so it means much less inside a bundle branch block.

16 tracings, each labelled INVT (inverted T-waves) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.
