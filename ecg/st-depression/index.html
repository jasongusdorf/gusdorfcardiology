Ischemia and infarction

ST depression

ST segment depression measured at the J point relative to the TP baseline. Horizontal and downsloping depression carries far more weight than upsloping. Diffuse depression with elevation in aVR suggests global subendocardial ischemia rather than a single territory.

16 tracings, each labelled STD_ (non-specific ST depression) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.
