Ischemia and infarction

Nonspecific ischemic ST-T change

ST depression or T wave inversion that does not localize to a single territory. The label is honest about its own uncertainty. It carries weight with symptoms and prior tracings, and very little without either.

12 tracings, each labelled ISC_ (non-specific ischemic) by the dataset's reviewing cardiologists. Click any tracing for its full read. Real patients, so most carry other findings too.
