About the work

Editorial standards

How this site separates evidence, teaching synthesis, original illustration, and clinical review.

Scope

Gusdorf Cardiology is free education for clinicians and trainees. It explains mechanisms, interpretation, and management frameworks; it does not provide patient-specific medical advice or replace local protocols, current guidance, or clinical judgment.

Evidence and updates

Lessons are built from major society guidelines, core cardiology references, primary literature, and canonical reviews. Each lesson shows its evidence-review date and links its principal guidelines and further reading. Recommendations can change; the dated review record makes that limitation visible without interrupting every lesson.

Clinical review

Jason Gusdorf, MD, authors and maintains the curriculum. Material should not be described as independently peer-reviewed unless a named external reviewer has approved it. Independent review status will be stated explicitly when available.

Images and media

Original atlas plates are identified as AI-generated medical illustrations and undergo clinical visual QA before publication. They are teaching aids, not validated procedural or surgical atlases. Third-party media retain creator, source, license, and modification records. Patient-identifiable material is not used.

Corrections

Clinically meaningful errors are corrected promptly rather than silently defended. Questions or corrections can be sent through Jason’s website.
