Audit dimensions: anatomy and spatial relationships; physiologic direction and timing; waveform morphology; imaging-plane/projection conventions; device course and mechanics; labels and internal consistency; agreement with the lesson caption; and whether synthetic diagnostic media could be mistaken for real clinical data.

### Severity definitions

- **Rebuild:** a material error, internally inconsistent visual, synthetic diagnostic image, or geometry that cannot be made authoritative with a caption change.
- **Correctable:** the core teaching model is reasonable, but labels, scope, or explicit simplification boundaries must change and the plate must be redrawn deterministically.
- **Pass:** accurate enough for use as a reference. **No generated plate passed this audit.**

## Replacement standard

1. Use deterministic SVG/vector graphs for PV loops, pressure traces, action potentials, ECG timing, hemodynamic tables, and causal pathways.
2. Use real, licensed/de-identified clinical source media for angiography, echo, Doppler, CMR, ECG, histology, and ultrasound; never generate diagnostic-looking studies.
3. Use established anatomy sources or commissioned/manual vector reconstruction for coronary, valvular, conduction, device, and projection anatomy.
4. Require a written source specification for every label, axis, arrow, phase, pressure relationship, and device landmark before drawing.
5. Conduct content review independently from visual review. A plate passes only when both the image and its caption are correct.
6. Restore a plate to the site only after approval is documented in this audit file with source citations and reviewer/date.
