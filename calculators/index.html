Decision pathways

From score to clinical sequence

Severe aortic stenosis

  1. 1Confirm severity and measurement concordance
  2. 2Establish symptoms and LV response
  3. 3Define anatomy, coronary disease, frailty, and life expectancy
  4. 4Heart Team choice of TAVR, SAVR, or surveillance

Acute decompensated heart failure

  1. 1Identify congestion, perfusion, precipitant, and shock
  2. 2Use IV loop diuresis for congestion; reassess urine output early
  3. 3Escalate vasodilator or shock support according to blood pressure/phenotype
  4. 4Before discharge: optimize foundational therapy, education, and follow-up

HFrEF foundational therapy

  1. 1Confirm phenotype, cause, volume status, BP, renal function, and potassium
  2. 2Build the four pillars: ARNI/ACEi/ARB, evidence beta blocker, MRA, SGLT2 inhibitor
  3. 3Titrate based on tolerance rather than waiting for target dose of one class
  4. 4Reassess EF, symptoms, iron, rhythm, devices, and advanced-therapy triggers

Atrial fibrillation

  1. 1Stability first: cardiovert if the rhythm causes instability
  2. 2Define rhythm/rate strategy, duration, triggers, and structural disease
  3. 3Assess thromboembolism and bleeding risks; address modifiable factors
  4. 4Reassess symptoms, AF burden, anticoagulation, and ablation candidacy
