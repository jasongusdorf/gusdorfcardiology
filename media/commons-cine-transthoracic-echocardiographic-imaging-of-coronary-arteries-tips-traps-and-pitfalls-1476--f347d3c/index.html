Reading order mnemonic: VIEWS

View & quality → Identify chambers → Evaluate function → Watch valves/walls → Seek measurements & missing data.

First name the acoustic window and view, then confirm orientation and image quality before interpreting anatomy. Sweep through the loop more than once: chambers and valves first, global function second, then the focal abnormality.

Source label vs. educational read

What the original source states

What in the image would support that label

  • The source identifies this asset as the finding described above. The available record does not provide enough adjudicated measurements to make a more specific patient-level interpretation.
  • Use the loop to practice systematic description, then return to the original source for the complete case and acquisition context.

Apply it clinically

This is a teaching example rather than a complete diagnostic study. Its value is pattern recognition; clinical conclusions require the full examination, measurements, and patient context.

Keep the differential open

What else could look like this?

  • •Normal variant or acquisition artifact
  • •Primary structural lesion
  • •Secondary change caused by loading conditions or another cardiac process

Complete the study

What data are still missing?

  • •A complete set of standard views
  • •Quantitative color and spectral Doppler measurements
  • •Loading conditions, rhythm, symptoms, and prior studies

Read it yourself

Before reading the source label, how would you describe this echo study in one sentence?

Reveal teaching answer →
Teaching answer: A strong answer names the view and modality, describes anatomy and motion without overcalling severity, identifies the dominant visible pattern, and states what data are missing.

Interpretive pitfalls

  • Do not infer severity, acuity, or etiology from a single selected loop.
  • The source label has not been independently adjudicated by this site.
About this interpretation

The source description is the asset-level clinical label supplied by the original publisher. This educational framework is derived from that label and modality; it is not an independent read of a complete examination. Measurements, absent sequences, acquisition quality, and patient context may materially change the interpretation.
