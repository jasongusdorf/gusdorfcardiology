Aortic bioprosthesis

Title: Aortic bioprosthesis Case Presentation: Description: PLAX: Color Doppler, mild aortic regurgitation

From Wikimedia Commons · Openly licensed teaching media

1. Orient 2. Observe 3. Interpret 4. Apply

How to orient yourself

First name the acoustic window and view, then confirm orientation and image quality before interpreting anatomy. Sweep through the loop more than once: chambers and valves first, global function second, then the focal abnormality.

Observe

Read it systematically

  1. 1Name the window/view and judge adequacy.
  2. 2Assess chamber size and global systolic function.
  3. 3Inspect valve morphology and motion.
  4. 4Look for regional wall-motion, septal, pericardial, and great-vessel abnormalities.
  5. 5Use Doppler measurements—not appearance alone—for hemodynamic severity.

Interpret

What the findings support

  • Inspect cusp coaptation and, if color Doppler is present, the origin and direction of diastolic regurgitant flow.
  • Assess LV size and function for chronic volume-overload consequences.

Apply it clinically

The study can demonstrate the regurgitant mechanism, but severity requires multiparametric Doppler assessment and clinical context.

Interpretive pitfalls

  • Do not grade AR from a single color jet or cine plane.
